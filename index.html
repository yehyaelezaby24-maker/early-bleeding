Part 1: Basics

Definition

Early (antepartum) bleeding = any vaginal bleeding after implantation (انغراس البويضة المخصبة في جدار الرحم) and before 20 weeks. It is the most common complication of early pregnancy, and it does not always mean a bad outcome.

⚠️ Viability (القدرة على الحياة برا الرحم) differs by source: 28 weeks (UK, older), 20 weeks or 500 g (USA/Australia), ~24 weeks (current practice). Use the number your doctor gives. Strictly, “antepartum hemorrhage” means bleeding after 24 weeks; before that it is “bleeding in early pregnancy”.
The 4 main causes (any bleeding comes from one of them)
  1. Abortion/miscarriage (إجهاض)
  2. Ectopic pregnancy (حمل خارج الرحم)
  3. Hydatidiform (vesicular) mole (حمل عنقودي)
  4. Local causes: cervical ectopy (احمرار وتآكل سطحي بعنق الرحم), polyp (زائدة لحمية), infection, dysplasia (تغير غير طبيعي بالخلايا), carcinoma (سرطان), ruptured varicose vein (دوالي), and subchorionic hematoma (تجمع دموي بين الغشاء والرحم).
💡 In pregnancy the cervix and vagina are very vascular (مليانين أوعية دموية), so even a small polyp or infection bleeds easily. ➕ First question to answer always: is the pregnancy inside the uterus or not? (ectopic = emergency).

Part 2: Abortion (Miscarriage)

Definition

Ending of pregnancy before viability. Spontaneous loss without any intervention is called miscarriage (إجهاض تلقائي). ➕ About 15–20% of recognized pregnancies end this way, mostly in the first 12 weeks.

Causes (Aetiology) (الأسباب)
  • Chromosomal abnormalities (خلل كروموسومات), e.g. trisomy (كروموسوم زيادة): at least 50% of early losses. The body expels a non-healthy embryo.
  • Blighted ovum (كيس حمل فاضي من غير جنين).
  • Maternal infection or any high fever; trauma (abdominal/pelvic surgery).
  • Hormonal: low progesterone (هرمون يحافظ على الحمل), diabetes mellitus (السكر), hyperthyroidism (زيادة نشاط الغدة).
  • Immune: SLE (الذئبة الحمراء) and antiphospholipid antibodies (أجسام مضادة تسبب جلطات): cause thrombosis (جلطة) in placental vessels, so the fetus gets no blood.
  • Uterine defects: septum (حاجز داخل الرحم), Asherman’s syndrome (التصاقات داخل الرحم), fibroids; over-distension e.g. acute hydramnios (زيادة السائل حول الجنين).
  • Drugs/environment, malnutrition, maternal anoxia (نقص أكسجين), old egg or sperm, stress, idiopathic (مجهول السبب).
  • ➕ Risk factors: age over 35, smoking, alcohol, heavy caffeine, obesity, previous miscarriage. ➕ Cervical insufficiency (ضعف عنق الرحم) causes painless loss in the 2nd trimester; treated by cerclage (غرزة تقوية العنق).

Types: the key is the CERVIX (عنق الرحم) + what came out

Missed abortion: details
  • Pregnancy symptoms fade (nausea, breast tenderness); uterus stops growing and feels firm; fetal movements stop; no fetal heart.
  • Pregnancy test turns negative ~2 weeks after fetal death; US shows collapsed sac.
  • Complication: DIC (اضطراب تجلط الدم في الجسم كله) after 4+ weeks because dead tissue releases clot-disturbing substances.
  • Treatment: often expels itself. Evacuate if not within 4 weeks, or bleeding/infection/DIC/anxiety. Uterus under 12 wks: suction evacuation (شفط). Over 12 wks: prostaglandins (PGE2/PGF2α) ± oxytocin (هرمون الطلق); hysterotomy (فتح الرحم جراحيًا) very rare. ➕ Modern option: misoprostol (دواء يفتح العنق ويطرد المحتويات) as medical management.
Septic abortion (إجهاض ملتهب)
  • Abortion (often criminal/unsafe) complicated by infection: E. coli, bacteroides, anaerobic streptococci, clostridia (بكتيريا تعمل غاز), staph.
  • Signs: fever + tachycardia (سرعة النبض); rigors (رعشة) = bacteremia (ميكروبات بالدم); low temperature with tachycardia = danger. Jaundice or haematuria (اصفرار / دم بالبول) = haemolysis (تكسير كرات الدم الحمراء). Rigidity and distension = peritonitis (التهاب الغشاء البريتوني). Foul discharge, tender uterus. Full painful Douglas pouch (الجيب خلف الرحم) = pelvic abscess (صديد بالحوض).
  • Treatment: isolate, semi-sitting position, IV line, vital signs, cervico-vaginal swab (مسحة) for culture & sensitivity (مزرعة وحساسية), antibiotics, fluids, blood if CVP (ضغط الوريد المركزي) low (normal 8–12 cm H2O). Oxytocin helps expel remnants. Evacuate after ~6 hours of IV antibiotics (earlier if heavy bleeding). Hysterectomy (استئصال الرحم) = last life-saving option.
💡 Antibiotics first, evacuation second: curettage on an infected uterus can spread infection into the blood.

Other terms: Therapeutic abortion (لسبب طبي); Criminal (غير قانوني); Recurrent/Habitual (متكرر) = 3 or more consecutive losses (investigate the cause).

➕ Rh-negative mother? (فصيلة دم سالبة) Give anti-D immunoglobulin (حقنة تمنع حساسية Rh) after any miscarriage, ectopic or evacuation, ideally within 72 hours, so she does not form antibodies that harm future babies.

Part 3: Ectopic Pregnancy

What and where

Ectopic = “in the wrong place”: the fertilized ovum implants outside the uterine cavity. Over 95% are in the fallopian tube (قناة فالوب), mostly the ampulla (الجزء الواسع من القناة). Other sites: ovary, cervix, abdomen, and ➕ the cesarean scar.

Why diagnosis is hard: early symptoms look like normal pregnancy (missed period, breast tenderness, nausea, frequent urination). The first warning sign is usually pain or bleeding.

Risk factors

PID/STI (التهاب الحوض), previous ectopic, tubal surgery, smoking, IUD (لولب), assisted reproduction (IVF). ➕ Also previous pelvic/abdominal surgery and age over 35. Inflammation and smoking damage tubal cilia (الأهداب), so the ovum is delayed and implants early.

Fate and numbers

The tube cannot stretch like the uterus, so either tubal abortion (الحمل يخرج من نهاية القناة) or tubal rupture (انفجار القناة) with internal bleeding. Abdominal pain 97%, vaginal bleeding 79%, tenderness 91%, infertility 15%. Persistent ectopic (نسيج متبقي بعد الجراحة) 5–10%. Ectopic causes 10–15% of maternal deaths.

⚠️ Rupture signs: sudden severe abdominal pain, shoulder pain (blood irritates the diaphragm = referred pain (ألم منعكس)), dizziness/fainting, rapid pulse, low BP, shock. Heavy internal bleeding can occur with only slight vaginal spotting, so never be reassured by spotting.
➕ Diagnosis
  • Classic triad: missed period + pain + bleeding.
  • Positive pregnancy test + empty uterus on transvaginal US + β-hCG above the “discriminatory zone” (~1500–2000 IU/L).
  • β-hCG normally roughly doubles every 48 h in a healthy early pregnancy; a slow rise suggests ectopic or failing pregnancy.
  • Free fluid in the Douglas pouch = bleeding. Cervical motion tenderness (ألم عند تحريك العنق) on exam.
Management
  • Stable, unruptured: Methotrexate (دواء يوقف نمو خلايا الحمل) then monitor hCG. ➕ Patient must avoid folic acid supplements, NSAIDs, alcohol and pregnancy for 3 months while on it.
  • Ruptured/shock: call team, ABC, O2, 2 large IV lines, fluids/blood, then surgery: salpingectomy (استئصال القناة) or salpingostomy (فتح القناة وإزالة الحمل مع الحفاظ عليها).
  • Rh-negative: anti-D.

Part 4: Molar Pregnancy (Hydatidiform Mole)

What it is

Abnormal proliferation of trophoblast (الخلايا المكونة للمشيمة) with swollen chorionic villi (زغابات المشيمة) that look like a bunch of grapes instead of a normal pregnancy. Cause: unclear, chromosomal problems.

FetusNoneMay have fetal parts

Genetics46,XX, all paternalTriploid (69 كروموسوم)
Clinical features
  • Pregnancy test positive; feels normal for the first 3–4 months.
  • Uterus larger than dates (أكبر من عمر الحمل), very high hCG.
  • Bleeding before the end of month 3 (spotting to heavy), sometimes grape-like tissue passed.
  • Severe nausea/vomiting (hyperemesis (قيء شديد)) and high BP early; no fetal movement or heartbeat.
  • ➕ US shows a “snowstorm” (شكل عاصفة ثلجية) pattern; ➕ theca-lutein ovarian cysts (أكياس بالمبيض) and hyperthyroidism can occur from high hCG.
Treatment and prognosis
  • ➕ Suction evacuation, then weekly hCG until zero, then monthly for 6–12 months.
  • ➕ Avoid pregnancy for the follow-up period (reliable contraception; hormonal pill is fine) so a rising hCG is not confused with a new pregnancy.
  • Risk: remaining tissue can turn into choriocarcinoma (سرطان المشيمة), but cure is almost 100% and fertility can be kept if the uterus is preserved.
  • Emotional response is the same as any pregnancy loss.

Part 5: Nursing Care (Prof. Mervat’s lecture)

Assessment & history
  • Time and amount of bleeding (count pads (الفوط): soaked pad per hour = heavy); pain type, site, severity.
  • LMP (أول يوم آخر دورة) and gestational age; obstetric history (G = pregnancies, P = births; “G1P0” = first pregnancy, no birth yet).
  • Risk factors for ectopic/miscarriage; symptoms of shock; emotional response.
  • ➕ Ask about passed tissue and ask her to save it for examination.
Physical assessment

Vital signs, general appearance, abdominal exam, pelvic exam only if necessary and safe (it can increase bleeding or rupture an ectopic). Rising pulse is the first sign of blood loss, before BP drops.

General nursing management

Rest and comfort; monitor vital signs and bleeding; establish IV access; prepare for tests; emotional support; privacy and dignity; collaborate with the team. ➕ Keep NPO (nothing by mouth) if surgery is possible.

Emergency management (heavy bleeding / shock)
  1. Call for help; activate emergency team.
  2. Airway, Breathing, Circulation; high-flow O2.
  3. Two large-bore IV lines (كانيولا واسعة); fluid resuscitation; blood products.
  4. Monitor vital signs closely; urinary catheter to measure urine output (best sign of organ perfusion (وصول الدم للأعضاء); aim above 30 mL/h). ➕ Raise legs, keep warm.
  5. Identify and treat the cause; prepare for surgery.
Patient education

Report any bleeding or pain immediately; no self-medication; keep follow-up visits; avoid heavy lifting, strenuous activity, intercourse and douching (غسل مهبلي) as advised; learn warning signs (soaking a pad per hour, severe pain, fainting, fever, foul discharge).

Emotional support & complications

Acknowledge feelings, calm environment, let her express fears, involve family/husband, refer to counseling (إرشاد نفسي). Avoid “you will have another one”: grief is normal. Complications: hemorrhage, infection, anemia, shock, infertility (especially after ectopic), emotional trauma.

Case scenario: 26 years, G1P0, 8 weeks, spotting + mild lower cramps

Possible causes: threatened miscarriage, ectopic, mole. Assess: vital signs, bleeding amount, pain, β-hCG, US. Interventions: rest, monitor, IV if unstable, support. Teach: danger signs, avoid intercourse and exertion.

Key points
  • Bleeding is common but never normal. Early assessment saves life and fertility.
  • Ectopic = emergency. Closed cervix = may continue; open cervix = losing.
  • Emotional care matters as much as physical care.
